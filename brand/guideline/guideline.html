What AERA is

A digital twin of the Kelana Jaya Line

AERA is a digital twin of the LRT Kelana Jaya Line in Kuala Lumpur. It runs every train on the published timetable at real time and predicts how crowded each train, and each car, will be, using ridership data from data.gov.my. Riders use it to choose a quieter train or car; control-room staff use it to see where the line is under pressure.

The logo has to work for both groups: friendly enough for a rider's home screen and serious enough for an operator's console.

The name

AERA

Advanced Ecosystem of Rail Analytics

The word is older than the acronym. In Latin, aera is the plural of aes, bronze: the bronze counters used to keep accounts. Late Latin used aera for the fixed point from which years were counted, which gave English the word era.

AERA counts riders car by car and marks the moment a train gets busy, so the name already carries the product's story.

What it means

The train runs through the A

  1. The A is for AERA. It is drawn, not typed: a flat top and straight legs, all one width, so it stays solid at 16 px.
  2. The train sits where the A's crossbar would be. The train is what holds AERA together. It points right, forwards: AERA looks ahead and forecasts.
  3. The windows, a cab window in the nose and a side window between the legs, make it read as a train and not a bar.
  4. The speed lines say real time: the twin moves with the timetable at Kuala Lumpur time.
  5. The navy tile stands for trust and operations. Its 16-unit corners match the cards in the app and the shape of a phone icon.

Construction

Drawn on a 64-unit grid

  • One module, m = 7 units, sets the apex width, the width of each leg and the height of the train.
  • The A is 33 units tall, from the apex at 15 to the feet at 48. Its legs lean 12 units across in 33 units up (about 70°).
  • The train runs from 31 to 38 and its nose ends at 56, 8 units from the tile's edge. The nose curve is 15 units long.
  • The speed lines are 2 units tall with 2 units between them, at 70% and 40% opacity on the tile.
  • The tile is 64 units square with 16-unit corners and runs from AERA Blue at the top left to AERA Navy at the bottom right.
  • Below 24 px, use the small version: no speed lines and no side window, shifted 2 units left to rebalance.

Clear space

Give it room

Keep a clear space of x on every side, where x is a quarter of the tile's width (16 units, the same as the corner radius). No text, edge or other logo enters it. Lockups use the same x, measured from the tile.

Minimum size

Never smaller than this

Mark, screen16 px minimum. Use aera-mark-small up to 24 px.
Mark, print8 mm minimum.
Horizontal lockup96 px or 25 mm wide.
Tagline lockup180 px or 45 mm wide, so the full name stays readable.

Lockups

The mark with its name

The wordmark is AERA in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, outlined, with tracking tightened by 1%. In the horizontal lockup its capitals are as tall as the A in the mark and sit on the same baseline, with a gap of x between them. On navy and dark backgrounds the reverse lockups drop the tile, which would disappear into the background.
